# Track C checklists

All checkboxes from the Track C plan, unchecked and grouped by section. Tick them here as I go; the plan stays unchanged.

## 1.1 Leadership and operations

- [ ] Write simple standard operating procedures (SOPs) for 3 recurring processes (onboarding, deployment, client handover)
- [ ] Set up weekly team rhythm: 15-min planning, Friday review
- [ ] Learn to delegate: list tasks only I do, hand over 2 per month
- [ ] Give structured feedback: monthly one-on-ones with each team member
- [ ] Track team metrics (delivery time, bugs, client satisfaction)
- [ ] Read: *High Output Management* (Andy Grove), *The Manager's Path* (Camille Fournier)

## 1.2 Project and product management

- [ ] Learn Agile basics: sprints, backlog, definition of done
- [ ] Use one tool consistently (GitHub Projects, Trello or Notion)
- [ ] Write a short project brief and risk list for each new project
- [ ] Practise estimating tasks, then compare estimate vs actual each month

## 1.3 Communication

- [ ] Write clearly: short emails, clear tickets, release notes
- [ ] Present: give one 10-minute talk (team meeting or tech meetup) per quarter
- [ ] Practise a 2-minute pitch for myself and for the platform
- [ ] Improve client communication: status updates every week, no surprises

## 1.4 Business and finance basics

- [ ] Learn to read a simple profit-and-loss and cash flow statement
- [ ] Build a spreadsheet: costs per client project, hourly rate, profit margin
- [ ] Understand pricing: value-based vs time-based
- [ ] Understand contracts basics: scope, payment terms, intellectual property, liability
- [ ] Read: *The Personal MBA* (Josh Kaufman) or *Profit First*

## 1.5 Marketing and personal brand

- [ ] Keep portfolio current: add case studies with problem > solution > result
- [ ] Update LinkedIn monthly
- [ ] Post one useful thing per week (a lesson, a mini tutorial, a project update)
- [ ] Collect testimonials after every project
- [ ] Ask for referrals

## 1.6 Teaching and content creation (my unfair advantage)

- [ ] Record 3 short practice lessons (8-12 min) on my own topic; review audio and clarity
- [ ] Learn simple recording setup: quiet room, phone on tripod, lapel mic, screen recording
- [ ] Learn lesson structure: hook, explain, example, quick quiz, summary
- [ ] These lessons later become sample content for the platform and training for teachers

## 1.7 Career direction (decide by month 6)

- [ ] Choose a career direction by month 6 (see [3-year direction](reviews/3-year-direction.md))
- [ ] Write one page: *Where do I want to be in 3 years, and what proves I am on the way?*
- [ ] Adjust this plan to fit the chosen direction

## 2.1 Money

- [ ] Track income and expenses monthly (a simple spreadsheet is enough)
- [ ] Build an emergency fund (target: 3 months of expenses, grow step by step)
- [ ] Separate personal and business money; open a separate account for platform costs
- [ ] Set a monthly saving/investment habit, even if small
- [ ] Learn the basics of safe investing available in Malawi (savings, treasury bills, unit trusts); compare options and ask a licensed advisor before big decisions

## 2.2 Health and energy

- [ ] Sleep 7+ hours on most nights
- [ ] Exercise 3 times a week (walks count)
- [ ] Take real breaks: 5 min each hour at the computer; one full rest day weekly
- [ ] Drink water, eat regular meals, limit late-night coding
- [ ] Watch for burnout signs: irritability, loss of interest, constant tiredness. If they appear, reduce the load and talk to someone

## 2.3 Mind and learning

- [ ] Read one book per month (alternate: tech, business, personal growth, fiction)
- [ ] Keep a journal: 5 minutes at night (what went well, what to improve)
- [ ] Practise focus: one phone-free work block daily
- [ ] Learn something non-tech each quarter (a language, instrument, public speaking club)

## 2.4 Relationships and community

- [ ] Protect time for family and friends; schedule it like a meeting
- [ ] Find a mentor (someone 5-10 years ahead in tech or business)
- [ ] Find an accountability partner and do a 15-min weekly check-in
- [ ] Join Malawi tech and education communities; attend at least one event per quarter
- [ ] Mentor others: one student or junior developer per month

## 2.5 Purpose and faith/values

- [ ] Write my personal mission in 2-3 sentences
- [ ] Define 3 values that guide decisions (e.g. integrity, service, excellence)
- [ ] Review them quarterly: do my calendar and spending reflect them?

## 3 Boundaries and side-project hygiene

- [ ] Read my employment contract for clauses on outside work and conflicts of interest; talk openly with my employer if needed
- [ ] Never use company time, code or resources for the platform
- [ ] Keep fixed build hours (e.g. 1.5 hr weekdays, 4 hr weekends) and stop on time
- [ ] Say no to new commitments unless they support the 12-month goals
- [ ] Decide deliberately whether freelance work competes with platform time
