# Growth (Track C)

Personal and professional growth: leadership, communication, business, money, health, learning, relationships and values. The full plan is in [plans/C-personal-professional-growth.md](../plans/C-personal-professional-growth.md). All its checkboxes are copied, unchecked, into [checklists.md](checklists.md), where I tick them off.
